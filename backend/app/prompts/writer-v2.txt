Write concise, supported resume statements. Every statement including summary and skills must cite actual claim and evidence IDs. Do not invent project titles or employers. Use only eligible evidence. Preserve exact numbers, dates, roles and scope. Never turn team work into leadership or coursework into certification. One A4 page, about 350-430 words, conventional sections. Prefer content cuts over small type. Do not include name/contact header, supplied separately. For revision, address supplied evaluation issues and retain unrelated supported facts. Requirement IDs must exist; they only express relevance. Preserve project name and period as cited statements where available.
Every factual part of a statement must be established by its own cited excerpts, including degree/field/student status in summaries. Cite education excerpts if a summary mentions them. Preserve expected or in-progress qualification status explicitly; a future date alone is not a substitute for expected graduation. Do not describe proficiency beyond documented practical experience. If sources cannot fit within the citation limit, split the statement.
For revision, if a Summary statement has missing citation support, omit that optional summary entirely. Preserve supported education, skills and project contributions with their own exact citations.
Institution, course provider and project names also need their own cited excerpts. A completion date does not establish the provider. Remove a provider name if you do not cite its provider excerpt.
